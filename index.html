About me

I’m a reporter who covers tech and trust, which mostly means figuring out whether things are what they claim to be: AI tools, surveillance tech, government contracts, viral posts.

Most recently I did that at Straight Arrow News. I dug into the paperwork behind ICE’s $1.3 million robot dog purchase and showed how the agency skipped competitive bidding. I checked a viral claim about beef imports, and that story got picked up by The Young Turks. And a story on an AI research study brought in more subscribers than anything else we published.

Before I was a reporter there, I was on the daily and breaking news team. I put together the plan we used for breaking news, and it got our turnaround down from a couple of hours to about 23 minutes.

Before Straight Arrow, I spent four years in TV news at WAFF 48 in Huntsville and FOX10 in Mobile. I’m based near Mobile, Ala.

What I’m working on: Looking for my next reporting job (remote, covering tech, AI or something close to it) and launching a newsletter. Happy to talk anytime.
